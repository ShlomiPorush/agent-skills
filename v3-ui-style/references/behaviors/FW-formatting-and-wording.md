# UI behavior rules (v3 style): Formatting and wording

Part of the `v3-ui-style` skill. Companion to `references/v3-design-principles.agents.md` (look, behavior principles, interaction rules, what not to copy). The skill's SKILL.md maps ID prefixes to area files. Paths are relative to the skill folder.

How to read:

- One bullet is one behavior with a stable ID (`DS-1`). A rule may name rules of other areas by ID; when your screen uses that part, read those rules too.
- **Pitfall** marks a behavior whose history shows a failed earlier attempt, a revert or a fixed bug. It says what went wrong and what fixed it. Do not skip it: it is the reason the behavior is shaped the way it is.
- `v3:` gives the v3 helper and `file:line` in the bundled code under `references/v3-code/`, a copy of the mailcow Logs Viewer frontend at commit `8caf6e4` (`ui.css` is under `assets/css/`). Start at the cited lines and widen to the whole function and what it calls when the bullet is not enough. The rules were first written against the earlier snapshot `deb075d`, then checked rule by rule against this code.
- Rules the interaction fixes changed (October 2026) start with what v3 does now and name its helpers; the part marked "Before the interaction fixes" describes the code before them. Where the two differ, the first part is the rule; details the first part does not cover still hold. A sentence that starts "Before the auto-refresh layer" describes older code in the same way: history, not the rule.
- The principles file's section "Interaction rules v3 follows since its interaction fixes" lists what those fixes added. Never copy anything listed there under "Do not copy".

## Formatting and wording

- **FW-1 Times: clock today, date before; "ago" with the exact time on hover.** A list row shows only the time ("14:05", or "2:05 PM" on the 12-hour clock) for today, with "today" computed in the app time zone, day and month ("16/10") earlier this year, and the whole date ("16/10/2025") before this year, each as Date Format and Time Format write it (FW-9). Relative times: under 1 min "just now", under 60 min "N min ago", under 48 h "N h ago", then "N d ago", rounded; future times clamp to "just now". The exact timestamp is always in `title`. Where the column already says what the time is, drop " ago" ("Held 3 h", "up 2 d"). v3: `formatListTime`, `formatAgo`, `uiAgo`, `formatTime`; utils.js:375-427; app.js:1432, 1727, 3681, 3971, 5364, 5676, 5762, 5820-5821.
- **FW-2 Timestamps and chart dates in the app time zone.** Every time on screen, every "day" and every time a user types or picks follows one app time zone: the server's (`/api/info` `timezone`, from TZ or Settings > Application, UTC when unset), never the browser's; a zone this browser cannot use falls back to UTC, and About names the zone and says when it is not set or not a known one. How a date or a time is written follows Date Format and Time Format (FW-9); the examples here are DD/MM/YYYY and 24-hour. Full timestamps and log lines are the date and the time to the second (`formatTime`), so the Logs page matches Messages ("14/09/2026, 10:12:05"); `formatTime(t, { seconds: false })` leaves the seconds out. Chart hours use `formatClock` ("14:00"; on an axis `formatClock(t, { compact: true })`, which writes a whole hour "3 PM" on the 12-hour clock). Dates are numbers, never month names: `formatDay` (day, month and year) in spans, tooltips, the strips' edges, the trend lines' words and the alert window's axis (its first and last column, with the time) ("21/10/2026, 06:00 to 12:00"; whole days "14/10/2026 to 16/10/2026"), `formatDayMonth` (day and month) on Mail flow's six-hour and daily axes; their bars are cut by the server at the app zone's own clock, so a day can be 23 or 25 hours. A day bucket the server sends, already a day in the app's zone, is written from the day itself (`formatDayKey`), so no zone shifts it: rate-limit day labels and DMARC days as day and month ("14/09"), a DMARC report's title with its weekday ("Monday, 14/09/2026"), the mailbox activity chart's days with a short weekday ("Mon, 05/10", CH-4). Phones shorten event times to day, month and time ("14/09, 10:12"). Logs accept unix seconds or ISO; unparsable values are shown as is. Pitfall: dates were written three ways (numbers in the browser's order, English month names such as "Oct 14", and a fixed DD/MM or DD.MM on some charts), and one table showed 1:05 PM while every other time was on the 24-hour clock; #494 sent every date and time through the format helpers (FW-9). v3: `formatTime`, `formatDay`, `formatDayMonth`, `formatDayKey`, `formatClock`, `appTz`; utils.js:447-661; app.js:554-572, 2709-2723, 2988-2995; alert-activity.js:153, 259; dmarc.js:52-56, 1495-1496; rate-limits.js:263-286, 615; logs-viewer.js:751; about.js:161-168.
- **FW-3 Counts come from one helper.** Every count is written by one helper with thousands separators (`toLocaleString()`) and the right singular or plural ("1,920 events", "1 device", "1 address" / "2 addresses"), so a caption, its confirm and its toast never disagree; never concatenate a number with a plural word by hand. A kind with zero is left out ("12 mailboxes", not "and 0 domains"). Abbreviate only where space is tight: log counts "1.2K" from 1,000; DMARC chart axis "12.5k" from 10,000 (`toFixed(1)`, ".0" dropped). Pitfall: #288 removed the thousands separator from message, facet and time counts, and #376 reversed that app-wide with one helper for number plus singular or plural word. Plural bugs were fixed on the way: "1 domains", "1 entries" (#377), "1 attempt" (#279). One figure is the exception: a network's size is a plain number ("Whole network, 4096 addresses"), because a separator there changed with the browser's language ("4.096" with German settings) and broke a test that expected "4,096" (#484). v3: `uiCountLabel(n, one, many)`, `rateLimitCountLabel`, `rateLimitBulkScope`; utils.js:312-316; security.js:355-357; app.js:841, 904, 908, 1703, 3069, 3380-3382, 3582, 3626, 4685, 5426, 5685, 5722-5724; rate-limits.js:818-838; logs-viewer.js:117, 913; dmarc.js:48, 449.
- **FW-4 Durations, sizes, percentages, scores and limits.** Use shared formatters only. Durations show at most two units with zero sub-units dropped ("2d 3h", "1h 5m", "30s"). Sizes read "1.5 MB" with trailing zeros removed (B, KB, MB, 2 decimals, "0 B"; a second formatter goes up to TB). DMARC rates round to at most two decimals (`Math.round(v * 100) / 100`, "97.35%"), the home pass rate to one. Scores show one decimal in lists and two in details, with "+" on positive symbol scores. Limits read as a blue tag "100/h" or a neutral "No limit" (frames s, m, h, d); details write "100/hour" or "None" (sec, min, hour, day). v3: `formatSeconds`, `formatSize`, `formatBytes`; utils.js:663-694; app.js:3970, 4197, 4219; dmarc.js:31, 347; rate-limits.js:185; mailbox-stats.js:516-519.
- **FW-5 Truncate with the full text on hover; name what is missing.** Names, subjects and addresses end in "..." rather than wrapping in list lines, with the full text always in `title`: ellipsis on `.ui-msg-sub`, `.ui-msg-from`, `.ui-q-who > div`, `.ui-log-svc-name`, `.ui-sec-bar-name`; widths `.ui-msg-to max-width: 16ch`, `.ui-msg-mid 22ch`, `.ui-qd-opts 280px`. The reading-pane subject clamps to two lines (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2; line-clamp: 2`). A missing subject reads a muted "No subject"; rate-limit history subjects cut at 70 characters. v3: ui.css:307, 362, 605-637, 1538, 2232, 2278; app.js:1434, 1439, 4198, 4676, 4758; rate-limits.js:607-617; message-details.js:911-913.
- **FW-6 Names shortened to what people say.** Display the human form and keep the raw form in the tooltip: containers read "postfix" not "postfix-mailcow" on tiles and in toasts; the server card shows only the hostname of the configured URL; the footer shows "v<version>" and "mailcow: v<version>"; a suppression stored as `/^.+@example\.com$/i` shows as "example.com" with a "Domain" tag and the raw pattern in the tooltip (anchored, legacy unanchored and wizard subdomain regex forms recognised, `\.` unescaped). v3: `_cleanRegexDomain`; app.js:622, 919-923, 978, 1072, 5306, 5333; spam_filter.js:665-675, 1254.
- **FW-7 Labels and tooltips say what, how long and what happens.** Buttons use plain verbs and say how long ("Ban 1w", "Ban 30d", "Ban 12h", "Ban permanently", "Fetch reports" instead of "Sync from IMAP"): the ban length comes from the rule's saved setting (not an unsaved edit), hours divisible by 168 -> w, by 24 -> d, else h, 0 -> permanently. Tooltips state the consequence ("Lift the ban; the rule leaves it alone for a week", "Never ban 1.2.3.4/32" (a single IPv6 address as /128), "Click to disable this rule", "Ban it for 7d; the ban is lifted then"). Every Security row has one sentence saying what happened with the outcome in bold ("On the denylist: **banned until removed**", "**2h left**, then Fail2ban lets it try again."); a Fail2ban network ban is a row named by its network ("Every address from 1.2.3.0 to 1.2.3.255 is blocked. **2h left**, then Fail2ban lets them try again."), and an address banned through it reads "Blocked with its whole network: **2h left**. Unban lifts the whole network.". List summaries are plain sentences ("5 messages: 3 waiting to retry, 1 on hold, 1 being delivered. Postfix retries deferred mail on its own."). Keyboard: tooltips via `title`. Pitfall: #386 first put the rule's ban length on its Ban button with "Ban for good" for a permanent ban, then renamed it "Ban permanently"; #433/#434 renamed "Sync from IMAP" to "Fetch reports" and its window to "Fetch history". Wording lessons: say what happened and what to do, "attempts" not "tries" (#367), drop "would" (#352), names that cover what a setting really does (#339), trimmed tooltips (#415). v3: `securityBanLength`, `securityDescribe`, `fail2banEntry`; security.js:310-414; app.js:1755-1759, 2337, 3568-3583, 3920-3925, 5217, 5224, 5739.
- **FW-8 Confirmations name the object, the count and the consequence.** A confirmation names the object, the counted scope and the consequence, with a button named after the action: "Delete "Ops Slack"? Alerts will no longer be sent there."; "Disable SMTP for x? The mailbox can still receive mail. Its app passwords will be revoked, so mail apps that sign in with one stop working." (the last sentence only when Outgoing spam revokes app passwords); "This deletes 12 DMARC reports and 3 TLS reports. This action cannot be undone."; "Let user@x send again straight away? This clears the counter they are stuck behind. Their limit stays as it is."; bulk ones state the count ("Release ALL 12 quarantined message(s)?"). Deletes, limit removal and SMTP disable are marked dangerous (`isDangerous`); counter reset and applying a limit are not. Irreversible ones add "This cannot be undone.". After Delete all, a 404 gives an info toast "<domain> has no reports left" and everything that shows reports refreshes. Keyboard: the shared confirm dialog (DS-12). v3: notifications.js:254-262; security.js:901-918; smtp-abuse.js:186-212; dmarc.js:1353-1413, 1384, 1520; rate-limits.js:626, 959, 1134; spam_filter.js:1115; app.js:3747-3788, 4028-4087.
- **FW-9 Dates and times are written one way, the one the user picked.** Settings > Application > Time has Date Format (Browser, the default; DD/MM/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYY, DD.MM.YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD) and Time Format (24-hour, the default; 12-hour; Browser); `/api/info` sends `date_format` and `time_format`, and `DATE_FORMAT` and `TIME_FORMAT` in the environment set them and lock the fields. Browser leaves the order of the date, or the clock, to the browser's language; every other choice reads the same in every browser. Every date and time on a page is written by the helpers in utils.js: `formatTime` (date and time; `{ seconds: false }` without seconds), `formatDay` and `formatDayMonth` (an instant's date, with and without the year; `weekday: 'short' | 'long'` puts an English weekday first), `formatDayKey` (a day the server names as `YYYY-MM-DD`, written as it is), `formatClock` (a time of day; `compact` writes a whole hour "3 PM" on the 12-hour clock, for an axis), `formatClockAt(hour, minute)` (a clock time such as a job's 02:00) and `formatListTime` (FW-1), all built on `writeDate` and `writeClock`. A page never formats a date for display on its own: no `toLocaleDateString`, `toLocaleTimeString` or `toLocaleString` on a date, no `hour12`, `hourCycle`, `dateStyle` or `timeStyle`, no `getHours()` and the like (the browser's zone), no `Intl.DateTimeFormat` of its own; `Intl.DateTimeFormat` stays inside the helpers and where a zone is checked or its offset read (`setAppTimezone`, the Time Zone list, MP-4). A test scans every page script for these and fails, with the allowed places listed by function. Dates never use month names. Where the year goes is decided per place, not by one rule: a list row shows the time today, day and month earlier this year and the whole date before (FW-1); Mail flow's axes, rate-limit days, DMARC report days and the mailbox activity chart (its columns and tooltips, with a short weekday) show day and month; Mail flow's spans and tooltips, the alert window's axis and tooltips, the strips' edges, the DMARC calendar's days and edges and a DMARC report's title keep it; the narrow Mail flow title drops it (CH-1). A date cell sorts by the instant it names, not its text: `uiTime(value, text, { title, cls })` writes a `<time datetime>` that the sorter reads first (TB-2), so a column stays in order in any format and through the hour a clock change repeats. In Settings an example under the two fields shows now in the formats chosen, before saving ("Example: 2026-10-23, 1:05 PM", `aria-live="polite"`); saving a change reloads the page, which reads them once; an unknown value is refused on save and never stops the app. Their descriptions say the choice and the default, nothing more: "Select your date format. Default: Browser.", "Select your time format. Default: 24-hour." Emails and the clock-time jobs follow the choice (EM-6). Not formatted: the API, CSV exports, raw log lines, "5 min ago" and durations, and the browser's own date fields. Pitfall: dates were written three ways and one table used the 12-hour clock among 24-hour times (FW-2); #494 added the setting, sent every date through the helpers and added the test so a new date cannot slip past them. v3: `formatTime`, `formatDay`, `formatDayMonth`, `formatDayKey`, `formatClock`, `formatClockAt`, `formatListTime`, `writeDate`, `writeClock`, `uiTime`, `settingsShowFormatExample`; utils.js:375-386, 550-662; app.js:213-215, 569-571; settings.js:255-256, 817-829, 844-851, 942, 1360, 1994-1995, 2145-2146, 2672; .github/tests/date-time-format.test.cjs.
