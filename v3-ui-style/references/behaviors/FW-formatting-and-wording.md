# UI behavior rules (v3 style): Formatting and wording

Part of the `v3-ui-style` skill. Companion to `references/v3-design-principles.agents.md` (look, behavior principles, interaction rules, what not to copy). The skill's SKILL.md maps ID prefixes to area files. Paths are relative to the skill folder.

How to read:

- One bullet is one behavior with a stable ID (`DS-1`). A rule may name rules of other areas by ID; when your screen uses that part, read those rules too.
- **Pitfall** marks a behavior whose history shows a failed earlier attempt, a revert or a fixed bug. It says what went wrong and what fixed it. Do not skip it: it is the reason the behavior is shaped the way it is.
- `v3:` gives the v3 helper and `file:line` in the bundled code under `references/v3-code/`, a copy of the mailcow Logs Viewer frontend at commit `ab0f644` (`ui.css` is under `assets/css/`). Start at the cited lines and widen to the whole function and what it calls when the bullet is not enough. The rules were first written against the earlier snapshot `deb075d`, then checked rule by rule against this code.
- Rules the interaction fixes changed (October 2026) start with what v3 does now and name its helpers; the part marked "Before the interaction fixes" describes the code before them. Where the two differ, the first part is the rule; details the first part does not cover still hold.
- The principles file's section "Interaction rules v3 follows since its interaction fixes" lists what those fixes added. Never copy anything listed there under "Do not copy".

## Formatting and wording

- **FW-1 Times: clock today, date before; "ago" with the exact time on hover.** A list row shows only the 24-hour time ("14:05") for today, with "today" computed in the app time zone, and day and short month ("16 Oct") before that. Relative times: under 1 min "just now", under 60 min "N min ago", under 48 h "N h ago", then "N d ago", rounded; future times clamp to "just now". The exact timestamp is always in `title`. Where the column already says what the time is, drop " ago" ("Held 3 h", "up 2 d"). v3: `formatListTime`, `formatAgo`, `uiAgo`, `formatTime`; utils.js:375-425; app.js:1222, 1505, 3174, 3435, 4767, 5079, 5164, 5221-5222.
- **FW-2 Timestamps and chart dates in the app time zone.** Every time on screen, every "day" and every time a user types or picks follows one app time zone: the server's (`/api/info` `timezone`, from TZ or Settings > Application, UTC when unset), never the browser's; a zone this browser cannot use falls back to UTC, and About names the zone and says when it is not set or not a known one. Full timestamps and log lines are 24-hour, day, month, year, h:m:s, 2-digit, browser locale (`formatInZone`), so the Logs page matches Messages ("14.09.2026, 10:12:05"). Chart hours use `formatInZone(t, { hour: '2-digit', minute: '2-digit', hour12: false })`. The Dashboard's Mail flow and the alert window's chart write dates as numbers in the order the browser uses, as `formatTime` does, never day or month names: `formatDay` (day, month and year) in spans, tooltips, the strips' edges, the trend lines' words and the alert window's axis (its first and last column, with the time) ("21/10/2026, 06:00 to 12:00"; whole days "14/10/2026 to 16/10/2026"), `formatDayMonth` (day and month) on Mail flow's six-hour and daily axes; their bars are cut by the server at the app zone's own clock, so a day can be 23 or 25 hours. Other charts keep their formats: rate-limit day labels are numeric ("14.09"; short form `DD/MM` elsewhere) so they follow the UI language instead of the browser's month names, and day buckets, already days in the app's zone, are labelled verbatim (dates, not moments); DMARC days read "Sep 14" and a day title "Monday, September 14, 2026", written as the day the server sent (`formatDayKey`), so no zone shifts it; the mailbox activity chart keeps "Mon, Oct 5" (CH-4). Phones shorten event times to "14.09, 10:12". Logs accept unix seconds or ISO; unparsable values are shown as is. v3: `formatTime`, `formatDate`, `formatDateShort`, `formatInZone`, `formatDay`, `formatDayMonth`, `appTz`, `formatDayKey`; utils.js:445-576; app.js:533-548, 2237-2248, 2510-2517; alert-activity.js:153, 259; dmarc.js:51-54, 1385-1386; rate-limits.js:212-237, 566; logs-viewer.js:751; about.js:161-168.
- **FW-3 Counts come from one helper.** Every count is written by one helper with thousands separators (`toLocaleString()`) and the right singular or plural ("1,920 events", "1 device", "1 address" / "2 addresses"), so a caption, its confirm and its toast never disagree; never concatenate a number with a plural word by hand. A kind with zero is left out ("12 mailboxes", not "and 0 domains"). Abbreviate only where space is tight: log counts "1.2K" from 1,000; DMARC chart axis "12.5k" from 10,000 (`toFixed(1)`, ".0" dropped). Pitfall: #288 removed the thousands separator from message, facet and time counts, and #376 reversed that app-wide with one helper for number plus singular or plural word. Plural bugs were fixed on the way: "1 domains", "1 entries" (#377), "1 attempt" (#279). v3: `uiCountLabel(n, one, many)`, `rateLimitCountLabel`, `rateLimitBulkScope`; utils.js:312-316; app.js:816, 863, 867, 1481, 2591, 2890-2892, 3091, 3118, 4141, 4829, 5088, 5125-5127; rate-limits.js:768-788; logs-viewer.js:117, 913; dmarc.js:47, 366.
- **FW-4 Durations, sizes, percentages, scores and limits.** Use shared formatters only. Durations show at most two units with zero sub-units dropped ("2d 3h", "1h 5m", "30s"). Sizes read "1.5 MB" with trailing zeros removed (B, KB, MB, 2 decimals, "0 B"; a second formatter goes up to TB). DMARC rates round to at most two decimals (`Math.round(v * 100) / 100`, "97.35%"), the home pass rate to one. Scores show one decimal in lists and two in details, with "+" on positive symbol scores. Limits read as a blue tag "100/h" or a neutral "No limit" (frames s, m, h, d); details write "100/hour" or "None" (sec, min, hour, day). v3: `formatSeconds`, `formatSize`, `formatBytes`; utils.js:578-609; app.js:3434, 3656, 3678; dmarc.js:30, 263; rate-limits.js:134; mailbox-stats.js:442-445.
- **FW-5 Truncate with the full text on hover; name what is missing.** Names, subjects and addresses end in "..." rather than wrapping in list lines, with the full text always in `title`: ellipsis on `.ui-msg-sub`, `.ui-msg-from`, `.ui-q-who > div`, `.ui-log-svc-name`, `.ui-sec-bar-name`; widths `.ui-msg-to max-width: 16ch`, `.ui-msg-mid 22ch`, `.ui-qd-opts 280px`. The reading-pane subject clamps to two lines (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2; line-clamp: 2`). A missing subject reads a muted "No subject"; rate-limit history subjects cut at 70 characters. v3: ui.css:307, 360, 603-627, 1467, 2161, 2207; app.js:1224, 1229, 3657, 4132, 4213; rate-limits.js:558-568; message-details.js:905-907.
- **FW-6 Names shortened to what people say.** Display the human form and keep the raw form in the tooltip: containers read "postfix" not "postfix-mailcow" on tiles and in toasts; the server card shows only the hostname of the configured URL; the footer shows "v<version>" and "mailcow: v<version>"; a suppression stored as `/^.+@example\.com$/i` shows as "example.com" with a "Domain" tag and the raw pattern in the tooltip (anchored, legacy unanchored and wizard subdomain regex forms recognised, `\.` unescaped). v3: `_cleanRegexDomain`; app.js:598, 883-887, 942, 1036, 4709, 4736; spam_filter.js:640-650, 1229.
- **FW-7 Labels and tooltips say what, how long and what happens.** Buttons use plain verbs and say how long ("Ban 1w", "Ban 30d", "Ban 12h", "Ban permanently", "Fetch reports" instead of "Sync from IMAP"): the ban length comes from the rule's saved setting (not an unsaved edit), hours divisible by 168 -> w, by 24 -> d, else h, 0 -> permanently. Tooltips state the consequence ("Lift the ban; the rule leaves it alone for a week", "Never ban 1.2.3.4/32" (a single IPv6 address as /128), "Click to disable this rule", "Ban it for 7d; the ban is lifted then"). Every Security row has one sentence saying what happened with the outcome in bold ("On the denylist: **banned until removed**", "**2h left**, then Fail2ban lets it try again."); a Fail2ban network ban is a row named by its network ("Every address from 1.2.3.0 to 1.2.3.255 is blocked. **2h left**, then Fail2ban lets them try again."), and an address banned through it reads "Blocked with its whole network: **2h left**. Unban lifts the whole network.". List summaries are plain sentences ("5 messages: 3 waiting to retry, 1 on hold, 1 being delivered. Postfix retries deferred mail on its own."). Keyboard: tooltips via `title`. Pitfall: #386 first put the rule's ban length on its Ban button with "Ban for good" for a permanent ban, then renamed it "Ban permanently"; #433/#434 renamed "Sync from IMAP" to "Fetch reports" and its window to "Fetch history". Wording lessons: say what happened and what to do, "attempts" not "tries" (#367), drop "would" (#352), names that cover what a setting really does (#339), trimmed tooltips (#415). v3: `securityBanLength`, `securityDescribe`, `fail2banEntry`; security.js:271-375; app.js:1533-1537, 2045, 3077-3092, 3383-3388, 4630, 4637, 5141.
- **FW-8 Confirmations name the object, the count and the consequence.** A confirmation names the object, the counted scope and the consequence, with a button named after the action: "Delete "Ops Slack"? Alerts will no longer be sent there."; "Disable SMTP for x? The mailbox can still receive mail. Its app passwords will be revoked, so mail apps that sign in with one stop working." (the last sentence only when Outgoing spam revokes app passwords); "This deletes 12 DMARC reports and 3 TLS reports. This action cannot be undone."; "Let user@x send again straight away? This clears the counter they are stuck behind. Their limit stays as it is."; bulk ones state the count ("Release ALL 12 quarantined message(s)?"). Deletes, limit removal and SMTP disable are marked dangerous (`isDangerous`); counter reset and applying a limit are not. Irreversible ones add "This cannot be undone.". After Delete all, a 404 gives an info toast "<domain> has no reports left" and everything that shows reports refreshes. Keyboard: the shared confirm dialog (DS-12). v3: notifications.js:254-262; security.js:862-879; smtp-abuse.js:156-182; dmarc.js:1243-1303, 1274, 1410; rate-limits.js:576, 907, 1081; spam_filter.js:1090; app.js:3237-3278, 3489-3548.
